faq

short answers.

No hedging. No "it depends." Straight.

any executor?

Needs Drawing, getconnections, hookmetamethod. Most modern ones have all three.

sky not changing?

Some games spam-set Lighting.Sky. The purge loop wins in 5-10s. Toggle off/on to force.

record macro?

F1 to start. F1 to stop. F2 to play. Loop toggle in Macro tab.

jerk tool legit?

Loads from pastefy.app. R15 and R6 variants. Errors show in notify.

add songs?

Custom ID input, or click Fetch to scrape robloxsong.com for 300-600 more.

save config?

Settings → Save. Load restores widget states via the lib's Save Manager.

ban risk?

Any executor carries risk. CFrame speed, real remote, tool-internal hooks. Use your head.

where do i start?

Copy the loadstring at the top. Everything else is inside the menu.
